My research focuses on Human-AI Interaction, Social Computing, and Trustworthy LLM Evaluation. I study how large language models, public AI agents, and intelligent systems affect human trust, privacy, fairness, emotional expression, and collaboration in educational, communicative, and social settings.

I have published or had accepted 21 conference and journal papers as first or corresponding author, including 10 CCF-A papers (9 conference papers and ACM TOIS) and 5 CAS Zone 1 Top journal papers. One paper received an ACM CHI 2026 Honourable Mention Award, and one was recognized as an ESI Highly Cited Paper.
