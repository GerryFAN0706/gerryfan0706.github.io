Oral presentation of [LePER: Label-Free Edge Polarity Reweighting for Heterophily](/publication/2026-icassp-leper) at ICASSP 2026 in Barcelona, Spain (May 2026).

LePER reweights graph edges by their estimated polarity without using node labels, so that graph neural networks can learn on graphs where connected nodes tend to differ (heterophily).

**Related publication:** Fan, G., Zhang, M., Zhao, M., & Pan, L. (2026). LePER: Label-Free Edge Polarity Reweighting for Heterophily.
